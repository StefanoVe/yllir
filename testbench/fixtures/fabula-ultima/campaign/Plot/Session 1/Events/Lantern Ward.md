_The breakwater lamp flickers, and the keeper's ladder creaks in the wind._

Kael Morrow climbs to the lamp room. The lamp needs oil before the next tide.

### Check: INS + WLP opposed

Kael Morrow and the lamp fight the draft for the flame.

#### Success

The flame holds and the lamp throws its light across the bay.

#### Failure

The flame goes out and the bay stays dark until morning.
