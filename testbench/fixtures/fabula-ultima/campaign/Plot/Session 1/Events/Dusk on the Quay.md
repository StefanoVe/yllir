_The gulls rise off the quay as the lamps are lit, and the fishing boats bang against the posts._

The party reaches the quay at dusk. The swarm circles the fish stalls.

### Check: DEX + INS DL 10

The party moves across the wet boards before the swarm settles.

#### Success

The party reaches the far end of the quay with the crates intact.

#### Failure

The swarm takes a crate and the party loses the morning's catch.
