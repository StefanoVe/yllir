_The iron sluice groans, and the water behind it drops a finger's width every minute._

Ysolde Varn waits at the sluice with a lantern. She wants the gate opened before the tide turns.

### Check: MIG + WLP DL 12

Lifting the sluice by hand takes all the strength the party has.

#### Success

The gate rises and the boats can leave the harbor.

#### Failure

The gate jams halfway and the water keeps rising.
