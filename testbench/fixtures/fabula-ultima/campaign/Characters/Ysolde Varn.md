# Ysolde Varn

## Role

Tidecaller who reads the sea for the fishing fleet. She knows which boats left the quay after the bell.

## Appearance and manner

A tall woman with grey braids and a coat of oilcloth. She speaks slowly and never raises her voice.

## At the table

Calm and precise. She answers questions with questions and wants the fleet safe before anything else.
