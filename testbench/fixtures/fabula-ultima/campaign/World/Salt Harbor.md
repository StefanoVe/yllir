# Salt Harbor

## Description

_Lamps sway on every mast, and the salt wind keeps the shutters closed until the tide turns._

Salt Harbor is the only working port on the coast. Its wooden quay runs out to a stone breakwater, and the tide comes over the lower steps twice a day.

## Points of interest

- The breakwater lamp, still lit by the keeper.
- The brine gate, an iron sluice that closes the harbor at night.
