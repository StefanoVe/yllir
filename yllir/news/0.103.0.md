## Your prepared sessions come over as sessions

When you bring over a campaign that is already in linked notes, the sessions you prepared now become sessions of the campaign, with one scene for each part of your notes, in the order you wrote them. The scenes keep your own way of writing them, checks included: the assistant does not rewrite them. If you want, it can later put them in the standard form, but only when you ask.

The assistant now also recognises more ways of marking your private notes, for example a heading that ends with "per il master" or a grey box that starts with "Per il master". Those parts go into the notes only for you.

Two smaller changes: on character and creature sheets, the assistant may put your statistics in the standard table, keeping every number, and it tells you which sheets; and it no longer adds short summaries you did not write. Your existing documents are not touched.
