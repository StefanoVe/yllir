## The atlas of your campaign

You can now ask for a map of your world: say «make the atlas», or press «Generate the campaign atlas» in the new Atlas section of the master screen. The assistant reads your documents, asks you what they don't say (how much of the world to show, where a region lies, a missing name), and draws the map in layers: land and sea, mountains, forests, rivers, borders, roads and places, in the look of a hand-painted fantasy map.

You approve the shape of the land once; after that it never changes unless you ask. When you add new places, the assistant puts them on the atlas by itself. Three looks are available, colourful fantasy, antique parchment and dark fantasy, and you can switch with a sentence. There is also a version for your players that leaves out everything you keep secret.

You decide only when the assistant asks. Your documents are not touched; the atlas is kept in its own folder of the campaign.
