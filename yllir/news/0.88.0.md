## Map markers show what kind of place they are

On the map site, every place now has its own small symbol: a star for a capital, a skyline for a city, a house for a town, a roof for a village, a tower for a fort, a broken column for a ruin, a diamond for a landmark and an anchor for a port. You can tell places apart at a glance, without opening them.

Your documents are not touched. Nothing for you to decide.
