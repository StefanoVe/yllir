## The master screen is easier on the eyes

The master screen no longer uses pure black on pure white. Pages now look like warm paper with dark ink, in both the light and the dark theme, and a single blue ink colour marks what matters: the main button of each page, the option you chose, and where you are in the side bar.

Each sign now means one thing only. What you selected has a blue outline. What the players are looking at has a blue bar and the label "Shown". In a fight, the creature whose turn it is gets the only dark row. Allies and enemies have different marks, with their names written next to them, and low hit points show a striped bar.

Documents, settings and the live table are easier to read. Small text is larger, and category headings stand out. Locks on master-only documents are easier to see. The settings are grouped into panels, and every switch says "On" or "Off".

You don't need to decide anything, and your documents are not touched.
