## The projector is faster and steadier

The projector window no longer stays black, and it no longer slows the master screen down. Switching from one picture to the next is smooth: the old picture stays until the new one is ready, then fades into it. Pictures you have already shown come back quickly.

When you project a fight, the players now see its battle map, without the grid lines drawn on it, with the tokens placed on top.

Your documents are not touched. Nothing for you to decide.
