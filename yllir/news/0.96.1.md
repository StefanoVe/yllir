## Every image can go to the projector

Images you open from a document now always show the Project button: in the Table, in the Documents page, in the full document page, in the plot map, and in the assistant's chat. Images on the live board with spaces or accented letters in their name can be projected too.

If you reload the projector window, it stays the projector instead of turning into the master screen.

Nothing for you to decide, and your documents are not touched.
