## Fewer questions when you bring over linked notes

When you bring over a campaign that is already in linked notes, the assistant no longer asks you one question for each way you mark your private notes. It decides how to read them (for example the grey boxes marked "secret" are notes only for you), tells you in the summary it shows before starting, and asks you once whether to go ahead: you can correct it in one sentence. It never repeats the text of your private notes in its messages.

If a note that holds several subjects starts with a few lines of introduction, those lines are not put on any sheet: they stay in your folder, and the assistant tells you which note they are in. Your documents are not touched.
