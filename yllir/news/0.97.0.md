## Sheets keep the players' side free of spoilers

The visible part of every sheet now holds only what your players may know. A secret purpose, such as a key that opens the door to the final dungeon, stays in your master notes. Characters, places, creatures, factions and events get a short synopsis at the top, written for the players. Every item also gets a type (book, weapon, armour, key and so on).

After the update I will go through your existing sheets with you:
- I will write the synopsis.
- I will move into your master notes where an item is found and any line that gives away the story.
- I will propose the type of each item.

I will show you the item types before writing them, and nothing is deleted: lines are only moved.
