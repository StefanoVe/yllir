## A few mixed pages no longer stop everything

When you bring over a campaign that is already in linked notes, some pages may mix notes only for you with text for the players. Before, if the assistant could not separate them well, nothing was saved. Now everything else is saved, and those few pages are kept whole as references: nothing is lost, and the assistant names them in its last message so you can go through them together when you want.

The sentences that are only for you are moved into your private notes word for word, and the move is kept even if the assistant has to prepare the campaign again. Your existing documents are not touched.
