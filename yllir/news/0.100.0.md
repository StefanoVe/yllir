## The assistant can check a rule online

When you ask a rules question and the assistant is not sure of the answer, it can now look it up online. It searches only with the name of the game and of the rule, never with anything from your campaign, and it tells you which site the answer comes from. If the sources disagree or it finds nothing, it says so and suggests checking the rulebook.

Nothing for you to decide, and your documents are not touched.
