## Bringing over a large campaign of linked notes is faster and cheaper

When you bring over a campaign that is already in linked notes, the assistant now decides folder by folder (for example "the notes in this folder are characters") instead of note by note, and the moving work is done by the framework itself. This makes a large campaign, with hundreds or thousands of notes, much faster and cheaper to bring over.

Notes that only you read, like the prepared sessions and the notes kept whole as references, are no longer checked for secrets, because the players never see them: only the sheets that can end up in the encyclopedia are checked. Links from a picture to another note, and links to a heading of the same note, are now kept. Your existing documents are not touched.
