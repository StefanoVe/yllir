## Maps of a town or a quarter

The atlas can now draw a single city, a village or a quarter, not only whole worlds. If your world is one town, or you ask for the map of a city, the assistant draws it from your documents: the river or the sea, the walls with their towers and gates, the main streets and squares, parks and fields, the quarters, and the houses in between.

Every building you have named in your documents gets a round marker on the map. Click it and a short summary opens, written only from the public part of its sheet, so nothing you keep secret is shown. The assistant asks you when your documents don't say where something stands, and never invents a name. You approve water, walls and streets once; after that a new tavern or temple finds its place without moving them.

The three looks (colourful fantasy, antique parchment, dark fantasy) work on town maps too. Your documents are not touched.
