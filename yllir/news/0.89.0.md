## The map site shows an endless sea around the map

On the map site, when you drag past the edge of the map you now see sea that goes on in every direction, instead of a flat beige colour. The sea picture is taken from your own map, so it always matches it, and it moves and zooms together with the map. If your map has no open sea to copy from, the beige colour stays.

Your documents are not touched. Nothing for you to decide.
