## You can hand over a whole campaign you already wrote

You no longer need to bring your old campaign in one piece at a time. Give the assistant your whole campaign, as notes, Word files, PDFs or photos, and it turns it into your campaign.

First it reads everything and shows you what it found, in plain words: the people, places, objects and factions, the past evenings it can recognise, and the things that do not add up. It writes nothing until you say yes. After your yes it writes the documents, the basic facts of the world and the past evenings, in small groups.

Your original files are kept exactly as they are. Unclear handwriting or details that contradict each other are asked one at a time, and you decide the answer.

You don't need to decide anything now, and your existing documents are not touched: they change only if you ask for it.
