## Fabula Ultima: fights, difficulty and checks

If your campaign uses Fabula Ultima, the assistant can now follow your fights turn by turn. Heroes and enemies take turns one after the other, and you say who acts; a champion can act more than once per round. It keeps hit points, mind points, statuses (and which attributes they lower), the Ultima Points of your villains and the Fabula Points of the players. It tells you when an enemy falls into crisis. It never rolls dice and never decides for you.

When you ask to rebalance fights, it now measures them the Fabula Ultima way: the enemies' level against the group's, and how many enemies there are, with an elite counting as two.

Checks in your scenes are now written as two attributes and a difficulty, for example «DES + INT DL 10», or as an opposed check.

Your documents are not touched. Nothing for you to decide.
