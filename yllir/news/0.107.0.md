## Yllir only steps in when you ask for it

The assistant now uses Yllir only when you are in a campaign folder, or when you clearly ask to start a new campaign or to bring over an existing one. In any other folder, or for any other kind of work, it behaves like an ordinary assistant and does not mention Yllir.

Nothing changes inside your campaigns, and you do not need to decide anything. Your documents are not touched.
