## Island cities, and the atlas waits for your answer

A world that is a single city on an island can now be drawn as one map: the island, its walls following the coast, the streets, the quarters and every house, all on the same page. Before, the assistant drew a plain map of the island with a dot for the city and kept the city for a separate plan.

The atlas window also had a flaw: after drawing the land it said the atlas was ready, but gave you no way to answer, even though you hadn't approved the shape of the land yet. Now it keeps the answer box open until you approve the land, so you can say yes or ask for a change.

Maps of towns are also lighter to move and zoom. Nothing in your documents is touched.
