## The assistant answers rules questions

When you ask a rules question about your game, the assistant now answers with what it knows of the rules. It says that the answer comes from its own knowledge, and suggests checking the rulebook when the answer matters at the table. Before, with some games it sent you to the rulebook without answering. If it does not know a rule, it says so instead of inventing one.

Nothing for you to decide, and your documents are not touched. The change applies the next time the assistant updates your campaign.
