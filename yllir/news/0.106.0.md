## Pictures in their place, and the mixed pages reviewed together

When you bring over a campaign that is already in linked notes, the assistant now gives each of your pictures its place: the picture under the title of its sheet, a map of a place under the sheet's map section, and a map of the world or of a region kept aside so that it can become the campaign's atlas, if you want.

The pages the assistant kept whole because they mixed notes for you and for the players can now be reviewed with you, one at a time: ask to go through one of them, and the assistant proposes which parts stay for the players and which go to your notes, without quoting them, and makes a sheet of it after your yes.

Two smaller changes: everything brought over is saved together, and the summary before starting describes your private notes only in plain words. Your existing documents are not touched.
