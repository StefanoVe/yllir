## Every mention of a subject now gets its link

When a person, a place or an object has its own sheet, the documents now link every time it is named, not only the first time or the full name. Doubtful mentions are checked by a second reader before they are linked.

The master decides only when a link would mean something the documents do not settle yet. Then the assistant asks one short question at a time.

After the update, the assistant goes once through your existing documents and adds the missing links; doubtful ones are checked by a second reader, and your text is not changed, only links are added.

You don't need to decide anything now. Your existing documents are only changed by adding links.
