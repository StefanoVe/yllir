## A damaged file no longer stops the whole import

When you bring several files into the campaign and one of them cannot be read (for example a cut or damaged PDF), the assistant now brings in the others anyway. It keeps the damaged file with your sources, tells you its name, and says clearly that the import is not complete. It never says that everything came in when a file is missing. When you have a readable copy, give it to the assistant and it brings it in.

Nothing for you to decide, and your documents are not touched.
