## The projector can show the fight

In a fight, the new projector button in the combat bar sends the fight to the projector window: the initiative order with whose turn it is and, when the fight has a battle map, the map with the tokens. It follows the fight by itself at every turn, wound or move. Click the button again to take it off.

Your players see only what they may know: enemies show a word for their health (unhurt, wounded, bloodied, down), never their numbers; heroes and allies show their hit points; hidden enemies stay off the screen until you reveal them. A battle map appears only if your public pages already show it.

Your documents are not touched. Nothing for you to decide.
