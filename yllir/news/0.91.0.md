## The sea around the map is now painted for you

The endless sea around your map is no longer a repeated piece of the map: it is a new sea picture painted to match your map, with the same colours and style, and it continues without visible seams. It is made when a map is finished, or when you ask for the sea around the map to be redone. Like other pictures, it asks your permission first. Until a map has it, the beige colour stays.

Your documents are not touched. Nothing for you to decide.
