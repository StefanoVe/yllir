## Fabula Ultima is now one of the games you can choose

When you start a new campaign you can now pick Fabula Ultima as its game. The assistant then writes characters and enemies with the Fabula Ultima statistics: level, rank, hit points, mind points, the four attributes as dice, defense and magic defense. It checks that the numbers add up, for example that an elite enemy has at least the hit points its level and Might give it.

This is a first step: fights cannot be followed turn by turn yet with Fabula Ultima, and the Italian names of the statistics may still change.

Your documents are not touched. Nothing for you to decide.
