## Start the atlas from a picture of your map

You can now give the assistant a picture of your map or of your town, or leave one among your sources, and ask for the atlas from it. The assistant looks at the picture, reads the coasts, the quarters, the walls, the streets and the buildings, and draws the atlas the same way as always, with the same look.

You still approve the land first, and then the whole map. The picture itself is never published and never sent anywhere. The painted version, if you ask for it, is made only from the drawn map.

Your documents are not touched. Nothing for you to decide.
