## Your session notes stay where you wrote them

Before, when the assistant saved a new session, it could rebuild the summary pages of other sessions, and the text you had written by hand on those pages disappeared from the page (it was still in the history). Now saving a session only touches the sessions it works on. If a session's summary page holds text you wrote, the assistant no longer replaces it. When a session does not say which sessions come before it, the assistant leaves it as it is, so it can ask you instead of filling it in.

You may be asked one question about where to keep your text: which scene of that session should receive it. Your documents are not changed without your yes.
