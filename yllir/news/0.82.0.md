## A painted version of your map

Once you have approved your atlas, the assistant can offer to turn it into one finished, painted picture, with the same coasts, mountains, roads, walls and buildings, just with a uniform, hand-painted look. It uses an outside image service, and it asks you first: that service only receives the drawn map, never any text or anything you keep secret.

The place names and the round markers you click for the summaries stay on top of the picture, so they keep working and always stay sharp. If you don't like the result, one sentence brings back the drawn map. If you change the map later, the page shows the drawn one again until you decide to repaint it.

Your documents are not touched.
