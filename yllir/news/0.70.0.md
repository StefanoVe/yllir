## The plot map is drawn as a flowchart

The map of the plot is now a flowchart that the assistant draws and keeps up to date after each session's work. It shows the sessions and their scenes, with the arrows that show what must come first and the branches that are optional.

The master screen only shows the map. It marks each session as played, ready or waiting, and a click on a node still opens its sheet.

After the update, the assistant draws the map once for your existing campaigns. The map is one new file in the plot folder, called "Plot map" (or its Italian name, in the campaign language).

You don't need to decide anything now. Your documents are not changed: the only new file is the plot map.
