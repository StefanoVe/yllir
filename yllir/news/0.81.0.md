## Cities inside maps, and scenery around cities

One map can now hold both scales. A region map can carry a detailed plan of a city, with its walls, streets, quarters and houses. A city plan can have forests, hills and mountains around it, drawn with the same pictures as the world maps, kept clear of the streets and houses.

When your documents don't say which cities deserve a detailed plan, the assistant asks you once. Water, walls and streets you approved stay where they are when something else is added around them. Your documents are not touched.
