## The Codex keeps hidden names out of its files

A small fix for the Codex. Two files of the site, which players could open if they knew where to look, contained the names of the sheets left out of the Codex. One of them also listed the entries that players had not discovered yet. Those files no longer go into the site.

Nothing for you to decide, and your documents are not touched. The fix applies the next time you update the Codex.
