## A new Codex page in the master screen

The Codex page is redone. At the top you see at a glance whether the Codex is up to date, when it was last updated, and what changed in your campaign since then. The preview of the site has its own tab, large enough to read.

You now choose what goes into the Codex from the same page:
- **Which sheets.** Places, characters, creatures, items, factions, events. A new campaign shows only items.
- **Entry text.** Show the text of each entry, or only its title and picture.
- **Which entries.** Every entry, or only the ones each player has discovered. A player discovers an entry by opening it or by typing its exact name.

The Codex now shows a grid of pictures, with the text under each one. An entry without a picture gets a small drawing of its kind.

Your documents are not touched: these settings only change what the players see. Your current campaign starts with items only. If you want places or characters in the Codex too, tick them on the Codex page and update it.
