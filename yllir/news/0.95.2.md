## A projector window for your players

The master screen can now open a projector: a second window you drag onto the screen your players look at. Open it with the new projector button in the side bar; double-click it to fill that screen. When you look at an image, the new «Project» button shows it in the projector, and the projector button bar lets you clear it again. Without a projection it shows only the campaign name.

Only images that your public pages already show, or that you put on your live board, can be projected: master-only images never get the button.

Your documents are not touched. Nothing for you to decide.
