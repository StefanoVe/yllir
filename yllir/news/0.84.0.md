## A new way to make the atlas

The atlas is now one painted picture of your world, with the names, borders and places placed on top. Before, the map was drawn piece by piece and often did not come out right.

When you ask for the atlas, the assistant starts from a picture you give it or one it finds in your sources. If there is none, it asks you before having one painted by an image service, using only the parts of your world that players may know. You approve the shape of the land first, and then the whole map. After that the land does not change unless you ask.

Later changes to a part of the map are repainted on a zoomed-in piece and checked before they are kept. The map still follows your world: new places are added to it when you create them, and the version for the players leaves out everything that is only for you.

Atlases made with the old system are left as they are and ignored: ask for the atlas again to make the new one. Nothing else in your documents is touched.
