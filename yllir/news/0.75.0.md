## Full names and calmer read-aloud text

The assistant now writes the full name of characters and places, so each one becomes a link. The text you read aloud, the lines of characters and the letters or notices you hand to the players avoid some stiff turns of phrase.

After this update, the assistant goes once through the documents it wrote itself, never the ones you wrote. It completes shortened names and rewrites those turns of phrase only in the parts you read aloud, the spoken lines and the texts for the players. Nothing else changes.
