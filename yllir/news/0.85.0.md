## ChatGPT can make your images

You can now ask for images (portraits, places, objects) through ChatGPT, if you have an OpenAI key. The assistant asks which ChatGPT image model you prefer, recommends the newest, and asks your consent once before using it, as it already does with the other image services.

Only the public part of the description is ever sent; your private notes never leave your campaign. The key stays on your computer and is never written in your documents.

Your documents are not touched. Nothing for you to decide unless you want to use ChatGPT.
