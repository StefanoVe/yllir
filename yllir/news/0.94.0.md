## More symbols on the atlas

Places on the map can now have a symbol that says what they are: shop, market, inn, workshop, mine, arena, tower, government building, temple, warehouse, pier, district and wasteland. City and district maps are easier to read, because a shop no longer looks like any other point of interest.

After the update, the assistant looks once at the places on your map that still have the generic symbol, and gives each one the symbol its sheet describes. A place whose symbol you chose yourself is left as it is. The map is saved as a new version, so you can go back. Your documents are not touched. Nothing for you to decide.
