## Bring over a campaign that is already in linked notes

If your campaign is already a folder of notes, one note per place, character or object, linked to each other (for example notes kept in Obsidian), the assistant now brings it over much faster. It moves your notes and splits the ones that talk about several subjects, but it does not rewrite them: your words stay exactly as you wrote them, and so do your links, which are pointed at the new places.

Lists generated from other notes and to-do notes are left out, and long reference texts are kept whole. Your original folder is never touched. Free notes and session diaries are still read with care, as before.

The assistant shows you what it will do, including how it reads your private notes and any two subjects with the same name or two notes that disagree, and waits for your yes. Your existing documents are not touched.
